# Required 3–6 minute video

Record yourself using the headset, with the headset view as the main picture and your camera in an inset. Upload to YouTube and put its actual URL in the README. This is a plan, not a completed video.

- 0:00–0:25: State purpose: learn about the furnishings and priestly service of the Tabernacle through an embodied tour and concise questions.
- 0:25–1:05: Welcome, tracked hands/body, small left-controller tablet, teleport and snap turn.
- 1:05–1:50: Altar and Holy Place, twelve loaves, oil lamps and incense altar; show Scripture references.
- 1:50–2:35: Ask an unscripted question, then a follow-up; capture guide speech, gaze, mouth and gestures. Try an unrelated question to show the subject boundary.
- 2:35–3:15: Wave and nod while guide is idle; show its acknowledgement. Show male/female child avatar selection and laver reflection; speak while holding X to show the visitor mouth.
- 3:15–3:50: Educational access beyond the veil, ark and viewing assistance.
- 3:50–4:20: Explain design choices and honest limits: no continuous camera motion, scriptural retrieval, robot representation, estimated lip sync. Show stable headset frame rate over representative areas if measured.

Before recording: check microphone permission and online service, volume, no stale replies, tablet readability, no arm/robe clipping, teleport safety and performance. Have another person playtest and record actual findings separately.
